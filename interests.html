Interests

Research Interests

The themes that connect my projects, and the directions I want to keep exploring in graduate research.

What Draws Me In

Additive & Sustainable Manufacturing

Process modelling and control of Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) systems, and the broader question of making metal manufacturing more resource-efficient.

Naval & Marine Engineering

Hull design, stability analysis, and vessel concepts for real operational needs — such as medical transport between island communities.

Mechanism & Biomedical Device Design

Low-cost mechanical testing rigs and assistive devices, like the below-knee prosthetic testing machine, where simple mechanisms solve real clinical problems.

Simulation-Driven Process Optimization

Using simulation and data to identify inefficiencies in production lines and propose practical, implementable improvements in industrial settings.

Applied Software for Engineering

Combining programming (Python, MATLAB) with embedded systems — from image-classification waste sorting to instrumentation — to make mechanical systems smarter.

Teaching & Mentoring

Time spent as a visiting instructor and society secretary has shaped an interest in communicating engineering clearly — something I hope to carry into research.
