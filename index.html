About

Who I Am

Mechanical Engineering graduate (Second Class Upper Division Honours) from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, with a strong interest in additive manufacturing, advanced manufacturing, sustainable manufacturing, and computational modeling.

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate (Second Class Upper Division Honours) from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. My final-year research project focused on the design and development of a Metal Inert Gas (MIG)-based Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) system, integrating mechanical design, process control, numerical simulation, and experimental validation to investigate metal additive manufacturing processes and their influence on part quality and performance. This work has resulted in two conference publications, including a first-author paper accepted for presentation at the 22nd Global Conference on Sustainable Manufacturing.

My academic and project experience has given me a strong foundation in manufacturing processes, mechanical design, finite element analysis, materials processing, and system modeling. Through industrial training at Hyundai Assembly Plant — Abans Auto, I gained practical exposure to manufacturing operations, production systems, process optimization, and engineering design in an industrial environment, strengthening my interest in developing innovative manufacturing technologies that bridge fundamental research and industrial application. I currently serve as an Instructor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Moratuwa, assisting with undergraduate lectures and conducting laboratory practical sessions.

My research interests include metal additive manufacturing, process monitoring and control, computational modeling and simulation, manufacturing process optimization, materials characterization, and sustainable manufacturing — particularly understanding how process parameters and material behavior shape final component performance. My long-term goal is to contribute to intelligent, sustainable, and high-performance manufacturing technologies through interdisciplinary research, and I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in additive and advanced manufacturing.

Design & Development of a Metal Additive Manufacturing System for Complex Component Fabrication

Jul 2025 — Jun 2026

Designed and developed a low-cost Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing system for fabricating complex metal components using MAG welding. Improved motion accuracy and process stability through control-system modifications, arc-length control, substrate cooling, parameter optimization, and thermo-mechanical simulation using ANSYS and MATLAB/Simulink. Validated the system experimentally through multi-layer deposition, dimensional evaluation, microscopic analysis, hardness testing, and fabrication of complex geometries.
