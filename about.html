Background

I am a final-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, with a strong foundation in engineering design, manufacturing, simulation, and naval and marine engineering. My work spans academic, research, and industrial projects, alongside experience in undergraduate teaching and laboratory instruction.

My primary research project is the design and development of a metal additive manufacturing system built around Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) technology, combining process modelling, control, and experimental fabrication — work that has led to two conference publications, including one accepted for the 22nd Global Conference on Sustainable Manufacturing.

Alongside this, I’ve worked on a concept ambulance craft for Sri Lanka’s northern islands, a low-cost prosthetic-leg testing machine, and several manufacturing and production-optimisation projects in industry — reflecting a broad interest in research, innovation, and the practical application of engineering principles.
